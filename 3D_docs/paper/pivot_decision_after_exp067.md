# Paper Pivot Decision after EXP-067

Date: 2026-08-26

Detailed evidence record:
[TTT adaptation-memory hypothesis retrospective](../analysis/ttt_adaptation_memory_hypothesis_retrospective.md)

## Decision required

The compact adaptation-experience thesis is exhausted on the competitive
CUT3R/TTT3R carrier. Continuing it would require changing several frozen
variables at once and selecting them on exposed failures, which is not a
defensible CVPR/ICLR path.

## What is established

1. Local online TTT can reliably descend a geometric self-supervised loss.
2. Raw, learned, exact-meta, routed, missing-evidence, coordinate-space, and
   function-space versions of the resulting adaptation experience do not show
   robust future absolute-3D utility on the competitive carrier.
3. Correct transport does not consistently beat untransported or spatially
   shuffled controls.
4. Explicit retained predicted surface content has a large causal advantage
   when current evidence is deliberately missing, including with predicted pose
   and native predicted scale.
5. Generic surface/feature memory is already occupied by Point3R, LONG3R,
   Mem3R, and related streaming map systems.
6. The first compact natural trigger—history-support provenance from predicted
   geometry—underperforms native confidence and worsens selective risk.

## Option A — Recommended: close this thesis and select a new paper problem

Start from a fresh 2023--2026 collision audit and require a strong no-fit
failure premise before architecture work. Do not preserve TTT-memory terminology
as a constraint. The new problem may still use CUT3R, VGGT, DINOv3, or D4RT as a
frozen foundation, but its central object must be independently justified.

Advantages:

- preserves scientific credibility and clean held-out evaluation;
- avoids a crowded explicit-memory paper with no deployable trigger;
- allows a compact one-mechanism design rather than accumulating repairs.

Cost: the paper thesis and experiment sequence restart.

## Option B — Higher-risk: pivot to explicit evidence sufficiency

Center the paper on when recurrent implicit state is information-insufficient
and a bounded explicit surface record is necessary. This requires both:

1. a natural, source-safe missing-evidence event—not the known synthetic mask;
2. a contribution distinguishable from explicit spatial memory, pruning, and
   retrieval prior art.

The first provenance trigger failed, so this path needs a genuinely different
problem formulation rather than a learned replacement trained on EXP-066.

Advantages: builds on the largest positive causal effect in the repository.

Risks: severe novelty collision, no current trigger, and likely expansion into
memory policy plus visibility/reliability components.

## Recommendation

Choose Option A for the CVPR/ICLR-first objective. Preserve EXP-057/058 as a
useful dissertation observation or diagnostic appendix, not as the current
paper method. Do not run another adaptation-memory or provenance variant on the
exposed 7Scenes contexts.
