What a streaming reconstructor forgets, and whether it can be handed back

Eight experiments on the fast weights of an autoregressive 3D reconstruction model, asking whether the adaptation performed over one stretch of a trajectory retains any value once the model has streamed through the next.

Model tttLRM AR · 24 layers · 169.9 M fast-weight params Data DL3DV · 35 scenes · 280 segment pairs Date 2026-08-11

Where it landed

Three claims survived measurement, three did not. The surviving three say there is something worth recovering and that it could be stored cheaply. The failing three say it cannot currently be recovered, and — more awkwardly — that what makes two adaptations interchangeable is which scene they came from, not how the camera moved.

The instrument

Two properties of the architecture make this model an unusually clean substrate, and the whole design leans on them.

First, SelfAttention runs inside each image — the block reshapes to (b·v/l, l, d) before attending — and the MLP is pointwise. The TTT fast weight is therefore the only path by which one view influences another. Adding a later segment to the input can change an earlier view's render solely through W, with no attention-context confound to disentangle.

Second, the AR schedule updates over input tokens in chunks and then performs a single apply across the whole sequence. Readout is separable from adaptation, so an arbitrary W can be substituted at render time without re-running the update — which makes sweeps cheap.

Together these predict that injecting the A-only state into the A+B run at full strength must reproduce the A-only condition exactly. It does, on every scene measured.

Plumbing check

α = 1 reproduces the reference condition to +0.0000 dB, on all 35 scenes. Any leak in capture, injection or batching would show here first, so this is verified before any result below is read.

Confounds blocked before measuring

  • Shared normalisation. scene_scale is derived from whichever views are in the batch, so an A-only batch and an A+B batch would be reconstructed at different scales and their PSNRs would not be comparable. Normalisation is computed once over the union of every view any condition uses.
  • Deterministic pruning. Gaussian pruning keeps a random fraction by default; set to zero so repeated conditions are bit-comparable.
  • Named views. The stock loader picks views by k-means. A, B and the held-out targets are specified explicitly instead.

Experiments

E1

Interference, and blending two adapted states

Forgetting confirmed

Query cameras, targets and Gaussian count are held fixed on segment A; only the input sequence changes. Because the scene is static, B adds information — any degradation is pure interference.

Interpolating between the two states collapses between them: the curve bottoms at 17.70 dB at α = 0.5, some 3.4 dB below the worse endpoint. The two adapted states are not linearly mode-connected.

E2

What A gains against what B loses

No usable trade-off

With query cameras spanning both segments, every α > 0 degrades A and B simultaneously. There is no blend weight at which recovering A is worth what it costs B.

E3·E4

How low-rank is the adaptation, and its difference

Rank-8 premise refuted

The bank was designed to store rank-8 factors. That assumes the adaptation — and especially the difference between two adaptations, which is what a bank actually holds — lives in a few directions. Differences can be far lower rank than their operands. Here the opposite is true.

E5

Correcting rather than substituting

Sign positive, magnitude negligible

A bank never swaps in a remembered state; it keeps the state the stream arrived at and adds a correction. That intervention behaves differently from the E1 blend — the collapse disappears and the slope at zero turns positive. But the gain is +0.045 dB against a 2.874 dB gap, and the B region gains slightly more than the A region it was supposed to restore. That asymmetry is what prompted the controls.

E5c

Four directions at matched magnitude

States are specific

Each correction is rescaled to the same Frobenius norm, so what differs is where it points. shrink moves back toward initialisation; foreign is the same construction built from an unrelated scene.

Three things separate. The B-region gain is not memory — shrink beats it outright, so it is de-adaptation, plain regularisation. The A-region gain is specific: only memory lifts it, and foreign actively destroys it, costing 1.04 dB where random costs 0.06. And the recoverable amount is negligible: 1.6% of the gap, or 0.5% once the part shrink explains is removed.

Without these four arms, +0.045 dB would have been read as a success.

E7

Localising the signal by layer

3 layers · 18 MB

The memory gain is too small to split 24 ways, so localisation uses the foreign penalty instead — 23× larger, and the same quantity measured where it is visible. E8a

Finding a descriptor

Only within scenes

Between scenes, nothing works: interference tracks the A-only PSNR at ρ = +0.83, and after partialling that out none of 25 candidate properties survives correction. One measurement per scene means the between-scene variance is scene difficulty.

Within scenes — eight cuts each, every variable centred on its own scene — 13 properties survive. But the split parameter itself moves the baseline quality at ρ = −0.86, so raw within-scene correlations up to 0.68 are mostly protocol. Partialling out both leaves a real but smaller effect, and only 24.6% of interference variance is within-scene at all.

The surviving descriptor also had to be made causal: the strongest candidate read the whole of segment B, which has not arrived when a skill would be retrieved. Reading only B's first quarter retains 98% of it.

E8b

Does the descriptor support retrieval

It does not

Correlating with interference is not the same as supporting retrieval. Twenty units — ten scenes at two cuts each — had their adapted states cross-injected into one another, restricted to layers 1, 2 and 7 at rank 8, which is the bank as designed rather than the full state.

Mean penalty by source of the correction
SourcenΔPSNR
the unit's own state20−0.319
same scene, different cut20−0.540
a different scene360−1.717

Own beats borrowed in 20 of 20 units (p = 4.7 × 10⁻⁸). So the states are specific — there is something to retrieve. But descriptor distance does not predict the damage: ρ = −0.042 over 380 borrowed pairs, and clustering the descriptor separates nothing at any K from 2 to 10 — the within-cluster penalty is consistently the worse one.

E8c

Is there an interaction a learned key could find

Mostly main effects

Before building an encoder, the penalty matrix was decomposed. If the damage is explained by main effects — some sources harm everyone, some targets are fragile — then the best source is the same for every query, which is a constant rather than a retrieval, and no key exists to be learned.

Variance of the 380 borrowed-pair penalties

target + source main effects68.7%

A bilinear key fitted on the causal features, validated leave-one-unit-out, reaches ρ = +0.064 (p = 0.14). Aimed at the interaction alone it goes negative and significant — the signature of fitting noise with twenty units.

The conceptual result

Whether two adaptations are interchangeable is governed by which scene they came from (+1.177 dB) more than by everything else combined (interaction sd 0.726 dB). The project's stated separation from content memory — store the adaptation strategy, not the scene — is contradicted by its own substrate: on this data, what determines reusability is content.

What this does and does not establish

The forgetting, the localisation and the specificity are solid: replicated across 35 scenes, 280 cuts, and out-of-sample on layers. The 18 MB storage design stands on 10 scenes it was not chosen on.

The negative results carry a caveat that matters. Every unit here is a different place — DL3DV scenes share no geography — so scene identity and motion regime are fully confounded. The claim the data supports is not that regime fails to govern compatibility, but that on this substrate the two cannot be told apart. A dynamic driving or egocentric benchmark, where the same manoeuvre recurs across different locations, would separate them.

One further limit sits under the storage result. Layer localisation used the foreign penalty because the memory gain does not decompose at +0.045 dB. That the gain lives in the same three layers is assumed, not measured, and is registered as a pre-committed check rather than left to be noticed later.

Finally, the oracle no longer functions as a kill test. It was run on a model trained greedily, so what it bounds is reuse without retraining — not the ceiling of a method that changes how the inner loop is trained. That makes the remaining question more expensive to answer, not less open.
